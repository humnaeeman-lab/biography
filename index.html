THE NOBEL PRIZE HISTORY

Nobel Prizes were established by Swedish inventor Alfred Nobel's 1895 will
and first awarded on December 10, 1901. Between 1901 and 2026, the prizes and the beonomics award have been given 639 times to 1,000 individuals and 28 organizations.

*ORIGINS AND FOUNDING

Alfred Nobel’s Will:
Nobel amassed a fortune from 355 patents, most notably dynamite, and left his estate to fund annual awards for the "greatest benefit to humankind".

First Ceremonies:
Awards began in 1901 on December 10, the anniversary of Nobel’s death.

Economics Addition:
Sveriges Riksbank (Sweden’s central bank) established the prize in economic sciences in memory of Alfred Nobel in 1968, awarding it for the first time in 1969.

Locations:
Most prizes are presented in Stockholm, Sweden, while the Nobel Peace Prize is presented in Oslo, Norway.

*CATEGORIES AND AWARDS

Physics: Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences: discoveries and research advancing understanding of nature and the universe.

Chemistry: Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences: significant chemical discoveries, methods, and materials.

Physiology or Medicine:
Nobel Assembly at Karolinska Institute: discoveries advancing life processes, disease understanding,and medical science.

Literature: Swedish Academy:
outstanding literary works and creative contributions.

Peace: Norwegian Nobel Committee:work promoting fraternity among nations, reducing standing armies, and advancing peace.

Economic Sciences: Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences: major theoretical or practical contributions to economics

*CRITERIA AND PROCESS

Recipients: Individuals only (organizations are also eligible for Peace).

Limits: Shared by up to three winners; no posthumous awards unless death occurs after the announcement.

Prize: A gold medal, diploma, and cash award.

Nominations:Nominations are submitted by qualified individuals and organizations, including previous laureates, professors, and members of academies.


Categories

Economics
